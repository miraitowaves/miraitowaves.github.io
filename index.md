## A Short Bio

I am currently an undergraduate student majoring in [Computer Science and Technology](http://www.cs.zju.edu.cn/) at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/). I also serve as a research intern at the [DCD Lab](http://www.cs.zju.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/d4/45/e46a2ca6469693738d84d1fffc3f/cc70a9ee-44ce-4603-9e46-701564a0eb2a.pdf), where I am fortunate to be advised by [A.P. Kun Kuang](https://kunkuang.github.io/) and [Professor Fei Wu](https://person.zju.edu.cn/wufei). Prior to joining the university in 2022, I dedicated my primary studies to Mathematical Olympiads, coached by [Mingliang Yang](https://www.hnsd2fz.com/teacher-development/faculty/25).

Currently, my research interests center on Large Language Models (LLMs) and Autonomous Agents, with a particular emphasis on Interpretability.
